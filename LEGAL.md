# Legal and permissions review

Reviewed 2026-11-07. This is a risk assessment, not a legal opinion or certification. No broker authorization, Terminal-specific agreement, exchange redistribution license or counsel approval has been obtained.

## Broker terms: unresolved material risk

[Ajaib's published terms](https://ajaib.co.id/legal), displaying an update date of 6 March 2026, section 12 items 5–7, restrict burdensome automation, automated scraping/extraction, and reproduction of protected material without written permission. Sections 5 and 12 address personal accounts, limited licensing and intellectual property. Reading only, owning an account or using accessibility APIs does not establish an exception.

The [official Terminal page](https://ajaib.co.id/terminal) advertises desktop access, while general terms retain mobile-device language. That discrepancy makes the precise applicable Terminal agreement uncertain; it does not cancel extraction restrictions. Confirm the applicable agreement and obtain permission covering this workflow before running it. Contractual consequences and enforceability require qualified advice.

The live-access opt-in is a technical control only; it grants no permission. No further live broker calls were made during this publication audit.

## Data rights and privacy

Publishing independently written adapter source differs from redistributing broker content. This repository includes no broker binaries, screenshots, logos, account records or historical market datasets. Numeric test values are synthetic. The MIT license does not grant rights to redistribute exchange or broker data.

Outputs may disclose financial records to the MCP client and its AI provider. Evaluate recipients, retention, cross-border processing and consent before use. Processing other people's data or offering a shared service needs separate assessment. Indonesia's [Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection](https://jdih.komdigi.go.id/produk_hukum/view/id/832/t/crc32/) is a relevant starting point; this review does not determine all applicable obligations or exceptions.

The name “Ajaib” identifies the intended interoperating application. The project claims no affiliation, sponsorship, regulator approval or rights in Ajaib's marks. No Ajaib branding assets are included.

Obtain qualified advice before production or commercial use. This report does not declare use or publication legally cleared.
